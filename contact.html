Frequently Asked

How does the rental process work?

Browse our collection, select your outfit and dates, and we'll deliver it to your doorstep. Return via scheduled pickup after your event.

What if the outfit doesn't fit?

We offer free exchanges up to 48 hours before your event date. Our styling team can also arrange minor alterations.

Is my security deposit refundable?

Yes, your deposit is fully refunded within 3-5 business days after we receive the outfit in good condition.
